Privacy notice

Updated 28 October 2026

SwiftShop Merchant is operated by SwiftShop OW. Contact: dancu1213t@gmail.com.

Information used

We use account and contact details, your store or worker profile, uploaded images, assigned orders, chat messages, and payment records to operate your workspace. Access is limited by your account’s role and assigned work.

Phone permissions

Camera access supports QR scanning and images you choose to upload. Location access supports maps and navigation when you use those features. This Android version does not request background-location permission. You can change permissions in your phone settings.

Notifications

When you enable alerts, we register a device notification token with Firebase Cloud Messaging, operated by Google. Alerts can arrive while the app is in the background, subject to your device settings and connection. A powered-off phone cannot receive an alert immediately.

Service providers and security

SwiftShop uses Supabase for authenticated data services, Google Maps for mapping, and Firebase for Android notifications. Internet traffic uses HTTPS. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details in order notes or support requests.

App health

We record aggregate app-version, loading-speed and error counts to investigate reliability. Error messages, chat content, addresses, precise GPS coordinates and payment details are not included in these health reports.

Retention and requests

Operational and payment records are retained to provide the service and resolve account issues. Order chats follow the app’s end-of-run deletion flow. You can request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated data by contacting SwiftShop. Some payment and business records may need to be retained after account closure.
