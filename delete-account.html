Account & data deletion

To request deletion of your SwiftShop Merchant account, email dancu1213t@gmail.com from your registered email address.

Include your account email, your store or worker name, and whether you also want your customer account removed. Do not send your password. SwiftShop will verify ownership before making changes.

Deletion removes account access and associated profile data. Payment, settlement and business records needed for unresolved transactions or recordkeeping may be retained. The team will explain any retained records when reviewing your request.
